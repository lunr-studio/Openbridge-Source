# Trademark and Asset Policy

“Openbridge”, the Openbridge marks, app icon, and menu-bar artwork are reserved Lunr Studio brand assets. Apache-2.0 grants rights to project-authored code and documentation but does not grant trademark rights.

Forks may truthfully say that they are based on Openbridge. A publicly distributed fork should use a distinct name and replace the Openbridge artwork so users are not led to believe it is an official Lunr Studio product.

Included reserved assets are:

- `Resources/OpenBridgeAppIconSource.svg`
- `Resources/OpenBridgeIcon.icns`
- `Resources/OpenBridgeMenuBarSource.jpeg`
- `Resources/OpenBridgeMenuBarTemplate.png`
- the generated app resources under `Sources/OpenBridgeApp/Resources/`

Openbridge documentation is Apache-2.0 unless a file states otherwise. Third-party models and packages retain their own terms.
