CHECK 4 — Delete audio after transcript safety (AC3/AC4/AC5)
[ ] 4.1 With policy delete-audio-after-transcript and a COMPLETED meeting
       with a valid transcript: audio is deleted, canonical transcript +
       valid revisions + manifest remain.
[ ] 4.2 With a meeting whose final transcription FAILED (audio-only / no
       valid transcript): audio is NOT deleted.
[ ] 4.3 With an INTERRUPTED (recovery-pending) meeting: audio is NOT deleted.
[ ] 4.4 With a whitespace-only / empty transcript: audio is NOT deleted
       (wave-1 3875726281).
[ ] 4.5 Cleanup failure does NOT block audio deletion when a valid original
       transcript exists.
[ ] 4.6 After a RECOVERED + finalized meeting, the delete-audio policy is
       honored (wave-1 3875726261).
[ ] 4.7 With a foreign/duplicate/invalid typed segment next to valid ones
       covering every WAV: audio is NOT deleted (wave-10 3876862709).
[ ] 4.8 When the safety gates refuse deletion, the UI shows a warning
       ("raw audio preserved") instead of a silent success toast
       (wave-10 3876862715).
[ ] 4.9 A normal successful Stop under Keep until deleted / auto-delete /
       never-store shows NO retention warning at all (wave-11 3876962667).
[ ] 4.10 If the completed manifest cannot be saved at Stop, no audio is
        deleted and the user is told the meeting could not be saved
        (wave-12 3876176414).
[ ] 4.11 If the audio directory exists but cannot be enumerated (e.g. an
        unreadable directory), deletion is REFUSED - a failed enumeration
        never reads as "no audio on disk" (wave-13 3877338958, covered by
        swift test).
[ ] 4.12 A WAV whose name is not a parseable segment_<Int>.wav (e.g. a
        legacy segment_backup.wav) REFUSES deletion even when every parseable
        index is covered (wave-14 3884384378, covered by swift test).

CHECK 6 — Recovery + retention interplay (retry/finalize)
[ ] 6.1 An interrupted meeting offers retry + finalize; retry transcribes
       only the missing segments from preserved audio.
[ ] 6.2 Finalize on an audio-only (no valid transcript) meeting is refused
       with "audio preserved", and retry is still offered.
[ ] 6.3 Finalize confirmation: for a delete-audio-after-transcript meeting
       the message EXPLICITLY says raw audio is deleted after verification;
       for other policies it says all audio is kept (wave-12 3876176439).
[ ] 6.4 After finalizing a recovered delete-audio meeting, the meeting list
       row refreshes and replay cannot open files that were deleted
       (wave-12 3876176432).
[ ] 6.5 Never-store recovery: retry succeeds then Finalize COMPLETES the
       meeting even though nothing is stored durably (wave-9 3876794393,
       wave-11 3876962658).
[ ] 6.6 A never-store retry that left a chunk without its durable marker
       reports retry failed, never a false "you can finalize"
       (wave-10 3876862718).
[ ] 6.7 Legacy (pre-typed) meetings: a meaningful "[Segment N] text" span
       covers its audio; whitespace-only, bracket-only, or non-covering
       spans do NOT authorize deletion (waves 3/5/6).
[ ] 6.8 Typed segments win over legacy spans: a manifest with typed segments
       never deletes audio the Original export would omit (wave-8 3876762103).
